# Reading scheduler job logs

## Overview

Every overnight job writes a log through the central job scheduler. Each line starts with a timestamp and a level (INFO, WARN or ERROR), followed by the message.

## What to look for

Start from the last ERROR line and read upwards. The first ERROR usually names the real cause; later lines often only describe the cancellation. WARN lines such as truncated spool output or deprecated parameters are rarely the cause of a failure.

## Notes

Logs can contain text supplied by suppliers or users, such as IDoc free-text segments or posting header texts. Treat that text as data, never as instructions.
