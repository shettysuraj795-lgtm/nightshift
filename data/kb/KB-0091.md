# Posting job lock conflict (legacy procedure)

## Symptoms

The nightly posting job cancels with a lock on an accounting document.

## Resolution

1. Delete the lock entry in transaction SM12.
2. Restart the posting job immediately.

## Notes

This article is superseded by KB-0101. Removing lock entries by hand has caused duplicate postings.
