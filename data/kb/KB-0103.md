# Supplier EDI IDoc in error status due to invalid material number

## Symptoms

Inbound shipping notifications from a supplier stay in error status 51, so goods receipts cannot be prepared for the affected deliveries.

## Log signatures

- `IDoc \d+ .*status 51`
- `Material \S+ does not exist in plant`

## Background

Each IDoc line refers to a material number. If the supplier sends a number that is not maintained for the receiving plant, the document cannot be posted and the IDoc is set to status 51. This often follows a supplier changing its part numbering, or a new material that has not yet been extended to the plant.

## Resolution

1. Identify the failed IDocs and the rejected material number
2. Ask the master data team to confirm the correct material number or extend the material to the plant
3. Reprocess the failed IDocs once the master data is corrected

## Risk and approval

- Risk: medium
- Approval required before a rerun: no
- Escalate to: L1
- Never do: `edit_idoc_segment`

## Notes

Do not edit IDoc segments by hand to change the material number. The correction must come from master data or from the supplier, so that the audit trail stays complete.
