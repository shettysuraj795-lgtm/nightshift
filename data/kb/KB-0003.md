# Rerun and approval rules for critical jobs

## Overview

Some jobs change financial or planning data and must not be rerun without approval: financial postings, MRP runs and any job whose fix involves a configuration change.

## Approval

Before a rerun, the job owner or their deputy approves it in the incident ticket. The operator records who approved it and when.

## Notes

Approval is never needed to check a job's status, read a log or escalate.
