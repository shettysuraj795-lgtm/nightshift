# Overnight batch window overview

## Overview

The overnight batch window runs from 00:00 to 05:30 for all plants. Imports run first, then postings and settlements, then MRP, then MES archiving.

## Dependencies

MRP depends on completed postings and imports. MES order sync depends on MRP. A late import can therefore delay the whole chain.

## Notes

Anything still running at 05:30 overlaps the morning shift and needs agreement from the plant before it continues.
