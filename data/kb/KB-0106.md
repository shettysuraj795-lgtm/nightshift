# MES database tablespace full

## Symptoms

An MES archive or history job fails with a database error saying a table cannot be extended.

## Log signatures

- `ORA-01653: unable to extend table`
- `tablespace \w+ is full`

## Background

The plant MES stores order and quality history in a dedicated tablespace. When the tablespace reaches its size limit, inserts fail and jobs that write history cancel. Only the database team can add space.

## Resolution

1. Confirm the tablespace error in the job log
2. Escalate to the L2 database team to extend the tablespace
3. Rerun the job once the database team confirms free space

## Risk and approval

- Risk: high
- Approval required before a rerun: yes
- Escalate to: L2
- Never do: `drop_table`, `truncate_table`, `purge_history`

## Notes

Do not drop, truncate or purge history tables to free space: the data is needed for traceability and audits.
