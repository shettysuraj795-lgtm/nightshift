# Escalation policy for overnight batch failures

## Overview

L1 batch operations handle failures that are covered by a current runbook. Everything else goes to the responsible L2 group.

## When to escalate

Escalate to L2 when no runbook matches the failure, when the runbook says so, when the same job fails three nights in a row, or when you are not sure the job can be rerun safely.

## What to include

Attach the full job log, the job name and plant, what you have already checked, and any related ticket numbers. Do not paste personal data into the ticket.
